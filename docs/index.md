## Overview

Removals Space Reputation Engine is a digital reputation management and online content analysis tool designed to help individuals, businesses, and digital professionals better understand and manage their online visibility.

The tool focuses on identifying potentially harmful, outdated, misleading, or unwanted content that may influence how a person, brand, or organization appears across search results and online platforms.

---

## Reputation Management Framework

Discover → Analyse → Assess → Resolve → Monitor

---

## Key Capabilities

### Online Reputation Monitoring
Track digital presence across search results, news, social platforms, and review sites.

### URL & Content Analysis
Identify and analyse URLs requiring reputation attention — categorizing by risk level, type, and required action.

### Search Visibility Tracking
Monitor SERP position and content visibility changes over time.

### Reputation Risk Identification
Surface potentially harmful, outdated, misleading, or unwanted content.

### Content Review Workflows
Structured workflows for systematic content assessment and categorization.

### Removal Request Tracking
Manage and track content removal request progress and outcomes.

### SERP Monitoring
Ongoing search engine results page monitoring and tracking.

### Digital Presence Assessment
Comprehensive digital footprint analysis across all channels.

### Reputation Reporting
Structured reports for reputation management workflows.

### Ongoing Monitoring
Continuous tracking of reputation metrics and changes.
